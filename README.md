## What does an internet TV subscription actually give you?

Think of it as moving the TV experience from your cable line to your internet connection.

With a typical internet TV subscription, you want three things to work together: the content library, the streaming quality, and the device you plan to use. TiviLive says its current plans include more than **28,000 live channels**, more than **100,000 VOD titles**, an electronic programme guide, sports and PPV access, and streaming quality up to 4K/UHD/FHD depending on the source and device.

That last point matters more than it sounds. A single connection can be fine for one TV. Once somebody wants the bedroom TV while somebody else has football on in the living room, the extra connections start making sense.

## One thing to check before subscribing

There is an important distinction with any internet TV or IPTV service: **IPTV is a delivery method, not a guarantee that every piece of content is licensed for redistribution**. What matters is whether a particular provider has the appropriate rights for the channels and content it offers.

TiviLive's current terms say the company acts as a reseller rather than a broadcaster and that content availability can change without notice.

So before paying for a longer plan, it makes sense to check that the content you intend to watch is available and appropriately offered in your location.
